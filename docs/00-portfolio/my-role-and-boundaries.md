# My Role and Boundaries

Use this file to record only factual, interview-defensible statements.

For every case, document:

1. The business or product context.
2. The exact problem I owned or contributed to.
3. Decisions, documents, experiments, or deliverables I personally produced.
4. Work owned by teammates or partner functions.
5. Evidence that may be discussed publicly after review.

Do not use "owner", "led", "from zero to one", or performance claims unless the underlying decision authority and outcome evidence are available.
